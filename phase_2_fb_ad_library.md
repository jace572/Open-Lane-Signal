# Phase 2 — Offer Bank from the Facebook Ad Library

Confirms competitors are spending real money in your niche, and produces a ready-to-use offer bank for your campaigns. ~5 minutes of human work.

---

## What's Going On Here

You're about to open Meta's free public **Ad Library** — a database of every ad currently running on Facebook and Instagram. You'll search for your committed niche, scroll through what's running right now, and screenshot 5–10 ads that have a clear offer (a specific dollar amount, a specific deal, a clear hook).

This step does double duty:

1. **It confirms the channel is alive.** If real businesses are running ads in your niche today — and have been for months — that's hard evidence Facebook Ads can profitably serve this niche. (If you find nothing or only stale ads, that's a signal worth raising before going further.)
2. **It seeds your offer bank.** Every screenshot is raw material. Once you submit them, AI extracts the offers and combines them with proven industry-standard angles to build a 15-angle menu of headlines, hooks, and offers you can use to write your first batch of ads instead of starting from a blank page.

You won't be doing analysis — just spotting ads with clear offers and snapping screenshots. The structuring happens after.

---

## The Deal

**You:** open the Meta Ad Library, search your niche, screenshot 5–10 ads that catch your eye, send them in the chat.
**AI:** confirm the niche is economically alive, then build you a 15-angle offer bank you can use to launch ads.

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1

Open any browser. Go to: **https://www.facebook.com/ads/library**

If a prompt asks you to log in, click **Not Now**. No login needed.

✅ The Ad Library page is open. Move to Step 2.

---

### Step 2

Set the filters near the top of the page:

- **Country** → United States
- **Ad category** → All ads
- **Active status** → Active
- **Platforms** → leave default (Facebook + Instagram)

✅ Filters set. Move to Step 3.

---

### Step 3

Click in the search bar, type your niche term, and press Enter.

A grid of ad cards loads.

✅ Ads are showing. Move to Step 4.

---

### Step 4

Scroll for 3–4 minutes. As you scroll, look for ads with **clear offers** visible right on the card — things like:

- A specific dollar amount (e.g., "$79 tune-up", "$300 off")
- A specific deal (e.g., "Free quote", "0% APR financing")
- A clear hook (e.g., "Is your door noisy or drafty?")

Take a **screenshot of any 5–10 ads** that have a clear offer. Don't overthink it — if it stops your scroll, capture it.

How to screenshot:
- **Mac:** Cmd + Shift + 4, then drag to select
- **Windows:** Win + Shift + S, then drag to select

✅ You have 5–10 screenshots. Move to Step 5.

---

### Step 5

Drop all the screenshots into the chat in one message.

✅ Done. We take it from here.

---

## What We Do With Your Screenshots

1. Confirm the niche has multiple active advertisers (alive economically).
2. Extract the offer angles you captured.
3. Layer in 5–10 additional industry-standard angles known to perform in this niche.
4. Send back a **completed offer bank as a Word document (.docx)** — organized into front-end loss leaders, trust angles, hooks, and scale-stage angles. Includes sample ad combos to test, formatted with proper headings, blockquotes, and tables so it's ready to read or print.

That doc is your starting point for writing every ad in this niche. Open it in Word, Google Docs, or Pages.

---

## Niche Verdict (Built Into This Step)

| What you see in the Ad Library | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Multiple advertisers, ads running 3+ months** | Niche is economically alive. **GO.** Real businesses are profiting from ads here, which means there's room for one more. |
| **A few advertisers, but mostly recent ads** | Channel is being tested but not yet proven. **GO with caution.** |
| **Almost no active ads** | Either the niche doesn't fit FB ads as a channel, or you're too early. **Reconsider** before committing. |

You'll see "Started running on [date]" inside any ad's details if you want to confirm — but you don't need to. Just eyeball the volume.

## Common Snags

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Search returns "No ads found" | Try a slight variation of your niche term (e.g., "garage door repair" instead of "garage door installation"). |
| Login prompt blocks the page | Click "Not Now" or close the prompt. The Ad Library is fully public. |
| Can't take screenshots on your device | Use a built-in tool (Mac: Cmd+Shift+4 / Windows: Win+Shift+S) or take photos with your phone of the screen. |
| Not sure what counts as a "clear offer" | If the ad mentions a specific number, deal, or asks a question that names a problem, screenshot it. |
